# Record the part

Take one 30–60 second video with the regular camera on Android or iPhone. Use bright, diffuse light and a plain contrasting surface. Fill most of the frame and keep the part in focus. Slowly show the sides, pause facing the main faces, then flip the part so the underside is visible. Pause on the faces that connect to other parts. Send the original MOV or MP4. Clear 1080p footage is sufficient, and 4K is welcome.

Take separate still photos with the calipers measuring length, width and height. For a round spacer, outside diameter, inside diameter and length are more useful than measuring the same diameter twice. Show the jaw contact points, reading and unit together. Zero the jaws first. JPEG, PNG and HEIC work. Existing photos can replace a video if they show the geometry.

For a blind hole, show the depth rod seated on its base. For a shaft flat, show both the round diameter and distance from the flat to the opposite side. For equal holes, center spacing equals far-outside span minus one hole diameter. Show how each span was taken. Ask for these only when that interface matters. When part of the original has broken off, show surviving fragments or the mating object if available.
